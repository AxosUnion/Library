# Mark-04 Kinetic Reactor
The _Mark-04 Kinetic Reactor_ is a type of reactor invented by Axos Akulonni-Katra and Alp Ak. It utilizes the variable nature of the sulfur cube (Ref)[https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sulfur_Cube] to generate energy inside of a controlled environment.

## Functionality
On startup, the reactor contains 6 sulfur cubes. The reactor is 5x1, so the extra sulfur cube is what keeps the tension perpetual once a flux has started. Once the reactor is ready, a sulfur cube is pushed, displacing all the others. Because of the lack of space, the sulfur cubes will never stop forcing against eachother, meaning that the conflict of space will be perpetual. Once the reactor is running, there are 4 important variables that must be watched:
- Flux (Movement)
- Tension (Stable force against eachother)
- Pressure (Damaging force against eachother)
- Polarity (Lack of equal distrabution within the reactor)
