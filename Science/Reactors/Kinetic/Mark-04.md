# Mark-04 Kinetic Reactor
The _Mark-04 Kinetic Reactor_ is a type of reactor invented by Axos Akulonni-Katra and Alp Ak. It utilizes the variable nature of the sulfur cube [https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sulfur_Cube]((Ref)) to generate energy inside of a controlled environment.

## Functionality
On startup, the reactor contains 6 sulfur cubes. The reactor is 5x1, so the extra sulfur cube is what keeps the tension perpetual once a flux has started. Once the reactor is ready, a sulfur cube is pushed, displacing all the others. Because of the lack of space, the sulfur cubes will never stop forcing against eachother, meaning that the conflict of space will be perpetual. Once the reactor is running, there are 4 important variables that must be watched:
- Flux (Movement)
- Tension (Stable force against eachother)
- Pressure (Damaging force against eachother)
- Polarity (Lack of equal distrabution within the reactor)

The following rules can help with managing the environment:
- High Tension = Low Flux + Low Polarity
- High Flux = Low Tension + Low Pressure
- High Polarity = High Pressure

## History
In late 2025, the AXU began experimenting with nuclear technology. They found success, however they only managed to manufacture low-yield nuclear weapons.

Once the AXR rose to power, the standing Civil Director (Alp Ak) decided to bring back the nuclear programme as he believed it had potential for superluminal travel. The rest of the Axian High Command was in agreement, so they constructed a nuclear weapon and tested it outside of the Democratic Republic of Brokenwood. It proved a success, and more was invested into the technology. Alp Ak and Axos Akulonni-Katra (AXR leader who gave him the AXU research) began working together more often, and after several days they conceptualized a superluminal accelerator. They called it the 'DRB FTL Drive'.

After lots of rigorous testing, a projectile was launched at a speed of ~200m/s which is still the current unbroken record. Since then several other designs of FTL system have been put forward but all of which failed to compare.

Upon the outbreak of the Hillysk War, all nuclear material was moved to the Phroganian Oblast in-case of a nuclear strike, putting off the FTL project. Which forced research into non-nuclear superluminal transport. After the Hillysk War, Alp and Axos began experimenting with sulfur cubes, and their unique properties. Eventually they came up with the idea of a _Kinetic Reactor_ which could in future be used as the energy supply for a _Kinetic Drive_.

Currently more research is going into kinetics, and it's applications in the race for superluminality.
